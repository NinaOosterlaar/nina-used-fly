# Pokémon in Copenhagen

We arrived at Schiphol in the morning to go to Copenhagen and join Pokémon GO Fest 2026.
A Go Fest is a special event in Pokémon GO, where players go to a city in either the US, Japan, or Europe to catch Pokémon and participate in raids.
It consists of a park experience, where you can go to a specific park in the city with lots of other players, pokestops, shiny boosted Pokémon, lots of gyms with raids, merchandise and other small attractions.
There is also the city experience, where you can explore the city while catching shiny boosted Pokémon and do raids. 
The city is usually swamped with players.
At Schiphol airport we already saw a lot of people wearing Pokémon merch, and we knew we were at the right place.
One of our friends also happened to take the same flight, so we met up beforehand before boarding the plane.

# Day 1

## Our Airbnb

We arrived in Copenhagen late morning, and took the public transport to our Airbnb.
So we already booked an Airbnb at the day that Go Fest Copenhagen was announced, but this Airbnb was cancelled 31 days before our arrival.
Airbnb compensates you if your booking is cancelled 30 days in advance, and then the owners also get a fine, but of course they cancelled it 31 days before our arrival...
My assumption is that they saw the rising prices of accommodations and wanted to earn more with their place. 
So we were very stressed about this, since most places were like more than 1000 euros for just 3 nighs which is way too expensive for us.
Luckily we found a new place added to Airbnb for only half that price and we booked it immediately.
So this is where we went. 

The Airbnb was really spacious, it was basically a whole appartment, but also very empty, there were barely any furniture.
Additionally, it was not very clean. There was a lot of lime scale in the bathroom and kitchen, and there also seemed to be some mold in the bathroom.
The whole bathroom situation was just very precarious, since it was very tiny. 
There was a toilet, and a sink, and attached to the sink was the shower, so it was all very crammed. 
The bedroom had a very small bed, but it was still big enough.
My main complaint of the bedroom was the lack of good curtains, but that is sadly enough more common in other accommodations as well. 
The neighbourhood was however very nice.

# Day 2

## Go Fest Park Experience

We got up at 7 at our usual time and got ready to go to the park.
Our closet metro goes straight to the park, and our station is before the main station, so it was still quiet. 
Every new station more Pokémon Go players started to enter the metro, until it became quite crowded.
Then we arrived at the metro station at the park and were immediately confronted by a huge queue to the merchandise tent. 
We skipped that for now and entered the park.

As usual, the park has Pokémon Go themed decorations, a huge Pikachu, a big team rocket R, some frames of the team leaders, and other random decorations.
In addition, there was a lego tent, which you could enter as well.
When it was quiet we entered, and basically it shows the Pokémon Lego, you could make a Lego piece of a map or something, and you could make a Lego badge, but that required another queue.
You could also take a picture with official Pikachu and Eevee mascots.
We already had a picture of the Eevee mascot from a few years ago, but since there was no queue for the Pikachu mascot, we also took a picture with it. 
The park was also quite big, so that was nice, but besdies that it was not a special park, just a lot of grass. 
But that is perfect for Pokémon Go.


The event was divided into four different sections.
One section spawned I think all Pokémon in the games (excluding of course legendaries and mythicals etc.)
This section was called the Conservatory Zone.
Another section was team Rocket themed, called the Team GO Rocket Hideout. 
I think Arlo was our designated team Rocket leader, since each Go fest had their own. 
The Recruitment Zone was themed around doing raids.
Then lastly you had the Cultivation Zone, which I think was more about field research and eggs and stuff but a little bit more vague to me.
And generally the theme was team Valor/Candela, since that was our designated team for the event.

Every hour you get a field research in each section.
So basically what we did is walk through all areas each hour to get all the field research. 
We caught a lot of shinies, and a lot of them I also really wanted. 
For example, Sebas and I both got a shiny rotom wash, a Pokémon that you can only get at Go Fest events.
At some point we went to the merchandise tent, stood in the queue for like 30 minutes.
Sadly enough the sweater I wanted was sold out for my size, but I got a Eevee t-shirt instead, and Sebas got all the previous Go Fest pins. 
We also did some raids, I did a few Mewtwo raids, until I got both a hundo Mewtwo and a shiny Mewtwo with background, so that is all I wanted.
There were also some Kyogre and Groudon, and Moltres raids, so I did some of those as well, but I wanted to save my raid passes as well.
So I used my free ones and only a few premium ones. 
Generally, the event was very chill, until the end.

We thought that mega Mewtwo raids would just spawn everywhere.
We did not read up on the event beforehand enough.
Apparently we got an email (that landed in our spam folder) where in the park we should go to to battle mega Mewtwo.
You see, for everyone in the game there would be two raids, one for mega Mewtwo X and one for mega Mewtwo Y.
The mega you would then catch can immediately mega evolve, you do not need the energy.
They distributed different locations for different players, so that not everyone would be in the same place.
We did not know that.
So naturally, when the mega Mewtwo raids started, we were on the other side of the park.
And I was confused why there were only two raids.
So we hurried to these two raids.
Meanwhile masses of people were raising there phone in the air, because the raid had this weird mechanic where you need to raise your phone in the air to catch it.
It looked very funny.
When we finally arrived we could still do the raid with some people.
But the lobby did not contain thousands of people like the raids that you could join if you were on time.
But I think you were not really able to fail the raid, so eventually we caught our mega Mewtwo. 
It was eventful.
Afterwards we ate some overpriced ice cream and continued on to explore the city.

## The Round Tower
 
The Round Tower you can climb to the top to have a nice view of the city. 
To get to the top, there are no stairs, but a spiral ramp that goes all the way up until you reach a final small staircase to the actual platform.
There is an entrance fee for entering the tower, a little bit on the high side, but what can you do. 
Going up the tower is really fun.
Stairs are usually super tiring, because of the repetitive motion, but the spiral ramp is a lot more accessible and fun to go up to.
You can also adjust your pace by walking closer to the middle wall or further away from it.
During the walk up you can see some old parts from the tower, and there was also a small exhibition.
I think our exhibtion was about scientist and art or something?
We did not stay too long, but Tesla was mentioned, the researcher not the car company.
At the top, you have these small staircase, which is really truly small.
There is a countdown timer, because the stairs so small that people can not pass each other.
So when the countdown timer goes to zero, we can go up.
When we finally reached the top, we had a very nice view of the city.
View points are essential for a city trip in my opinion.
Afterwards, going down was really fun again.
Because of the spiral ramp, it is really easy and you can go super fast.
We had great fun!

## Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek

Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek is an art museum in Copenhagen, which was founded by the brewer Carl Jacobsen, the son of the founder of the Carlsberg Breweries.
Carl Jacobsen was a passionate art collector, and he wanted to share his collection with the public.
Basically, the museum is a collection of art and antiquities that this guy was interesed in. 
It contains sculptures, and painting from different periods and cultures, including ancient Egypt, Greece, and Rome, as well as French and Danish art from the 19th and 20th centuries.
