After finishing my thesis, I would start an internship twee weeks later. 
I did want to relax a bit before starting my internship, together with my boyfriend. 
So we decided to rent a cabin in the Veluwe for a long weekend during Whit Monday.
The Veluwe is the largest forest area in the Netherlands, and spans across a large part of the province of Gelderland.
We found a nice cozy cabin at the edge of the forest in a small village called Rheden, close to the city Arnhem.
So that is where we went. 

First of all, our train trip to Rheden was very eventful in a bad way.
We took a direct train from Delft to Arnhem, but at Amsterdam Bijlmer Arena, our train hit a person.
Basically what happened was that the train does not stop at Amsterdam Bijlmer Arena, but goes through it with a high speed.
This man was sitting in a wheelchair on the platform near the tracks, and was sucked in by the train.
He sadly died, and our train made a big emergency stop. 
Luckily, we did not hear it happen, but we spoke to other people who did.
In the end we were dropped at a station called Abcoude.
There we took an Uber with two other people to Utrecht.
From Utrecht we took another train to Arnhem, were we had a quick dinner. 
Then we took our final train to Rheden and arrived signifcantly later than expected. 
We were very tired.

Our cabin was very cute.
You can see the pictures below. 
The cabin was located in a park with a lot of other cabins, called "Lodges op de Posbank".
Posbank is the name of the area where the park is located. 
Our cabin was located at the corner of the park.
That meant that we had only one neighbor, our other neighbor was the forest.
There is a picnic table outside where you can eat, and some additional folding chairs and a hammock, so you could enjoy the outside.
(Have to admit, at least in period we were there in May), there were too many insects to truly enjoy relaxing outside, and we mainly chilled in the doorway.)
Additionally, there was a screen placed around our cabin, providing us with even more privacy. 
The cabin is very small, which makes it super cute.
All the necessities were there, except an oven which was the only thing we missed.
Additionally, some places were a bit dusty, but overall it was clean. 
The park is located very near Rheden, it is around an 8 minute bike ride to the center of Rheden.
We did need to rent an OV-fiets for these days though. 

Our first day there, we decided to go for a walk at the Posbank, after getting some groceries first.
The Posbank is an area in the Veluwe that is known for its purple heath fields.
The heath fields were not blooming in May (they bloom in August), but the area was still very nice.
The first part of the walk goes through the forest, and then you get to the heath fields.
The main struggly however, was the hot weather. Because we are in a field, not the forest, there was not a lot of shade, making the walk quite exhausing.
The walk was around 5 kilometers, and when we got back we were actually exhausted. 
But we did enjoy the walk and the views were really nice!

We then chilled in the cabin for a bit, until we went to a nice restaurant closeby.
We got a place at the terrace, which was nice in the weather. 
We first eat some snacks and drinks, and then orders our main course.
I ate spareribs which were really good and Sebas ate one of his favorite Caesar salads that he ever had.
Then we eat a nice desert. 
Afterwards we went back to the cabin, made a plan for the next day and went to sleep.

The next day, we rented a e-tandem, for a bike ride through the area.
It was the hottest day of our stay, around 30 degrees, and there are quite some hills in the Veluwe, so the tandem being electric was essential. 
Sebas still says he had to put in effort, but I was vibing (I was on the back).
We looked up a nice route beforehand called "Rondje Posbank".
It is 44.3km and goes from Rheden to Velp to Dieren back to Rheden.
This route goes mainly through the forest but also passed some farmlands and through the villages, so it was quite varied.
It also had some highlights. 
First of all we passed castle Rosendael, which is a castle with a nice park around it.
We ordered a drink here but did not enter the park because we thought it was too expensive. 
We also drinked something at de Carolinahoeve, which is a nice restaurant in the forest, which looked family-owned.
It was very busy and chill vibes. 
We mainly stopped here because we needed to use the bathroom.
Then it was quite a long ride through the forest and the farmlands until we reached Domein Hof te Dieren Wijngaard, which is a vineyard in the middle of the Veluwe.
This was pretty cool, and we bought some honey here, since we do not really drink wine.
I recommend this place!
We also wanted to go to Landgoed Middachten, but that was under construction. 
So lastly we went to Landgoed Rhederoord, however there was a wedding taking place there, so we could't really enter the park.
We also did some drinks at a restaurant closeby, but that restaraunt was kinda odd vibes.
I guess it really tried to be fancy, but we were a sweaty mess so it was not really the place for us.
Then we cycled the last part back to Rheden, handed in our tandem, and went back to the cabin after a very tiring day.

After chilling a bit at our cabin, we went to the "Schaapskooi", which is a farm with lots of sheep that is subsidized, since the sheep do not make a profit anymore.
From our cabin we often heard a lot of sheep, and the the Schaapskooi is the reason why.
I think sometimes a herder does go walk the sheep around the area, but we did not see that.
When we visited the sheep where just chilling in the field, making a lot of noises that just sometimes sound like a person pretending to be a sheep.
There were also some young sheep which were cute.
A lot of the sheep were also recently shaped, which made them look very funny.
The funny thing is that we were so close to the Schaapskooi during our walk on day 1, but we did not notice at first, I don't know if we just missed it or if the sheep were gone.
But in general if you are waling in the area, it is a cute place to visit!

In the evening we decided to eat pancakes at the local pancake house.
This place was also family owned I think.
It was fun, there were a lot of colours and clowns? and painting that you could buy that I think were made by the owner (the quality was varying).
The people eating in the pancake house were very diverse, from families, to old people, to people our age.
We both ordered a pancake with a lot of different toppings, making it almost look like a pizza.
We however did not stay for dessert but went into the village to get some ice cream at an ice cream place which was nice.
Great end of a busy day.

On our last day we planned a 10km walk that goes through the forest, not the heath field because it was very hot this day as well.
This walk was very peaceful and nice. 
Just walking through all the trees, providing nice shade in this hot weather.
Only a few other people around us.
We saw one couple with multiple dogs multiple times, and at some point the woman fell, and it looked very dramatic at first.
Luckily she could get up and later in the day we saw them again, so she is fine!
The trail also goes past a small pond next to another castle or something, idk we didnt go to the castle.
The pond was very peaceful except for all the families with children vibing there.
But the sigh was nice.
At some point there was also a very loud group of girls walking behind us, which kinda ruined the vibe.
However, we just stayed still for a while and let them pass to find the peace again.
We only walked the wrong way once, becoming numbered signs experts!

We left quite early, so we finished quite early as well, but after 10km walking in still warm weather we were tired.
So the rest of the day we just mostly read our books at our cabin.
This time, to save money, we cooked a simple meal and ate it at the picnic table outside. 
It was all very vibes. 
During this time, we also saw a lot of animals around our cabin.
Basically, during our whole trip we saw a lot of squirrels, different birds, and some rabbits around our cabin.
All super cute, I loved watching them. 
But there was one important thing left to do.

At sunset, we went to the heath fields again, because according to the internet, animals show up at that time.
We were a bit skeptical, but it was only a 15 minute walk.
Just as Sebas concluded this trip is pointless, we saw a deer with a baby!!!
First I thought it was a hare, but omg I was so happy to see them.
We slowly and quietely tried to follow them and watch them as long as possible. 
I made some great pixelated pictures of them, since we of course kept our distance.
You can see them below haha.
We then continued on and saw some wild horses in the distance as well, which was cool.
At some point we saw some swines as well in the distance, and that is when Sebas became kind of scared.
Swines can be quite aggressive, and we were walking on a path with a lot of bushes on the side, so we did not see a lot of the area around us, which made him jumpy.
So we decided to go home again.
On the way home we saw a badger. 
Apparently it is very rare to see a badger, so again I was so hyped.
Sadly I couldn't make a picture, since it saw us and of course immediately ran away, but trust me we saw it!
This whole walk around the Posbank was amainzg and I was so glad we did it. 
I loved seeing all the animals. 
A great ending to our trip!
